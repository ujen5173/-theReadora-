# Vision & Future Roadmap

**Goal:** Create a platform where authors love to post and readers love to engage.

## 🚀 Growth & Engagement Strategies
- **Contest System:** Run contests for authors to submit work with prizes (Detailed below).
- **Outreach:** Reach out to authors directly via email/social media.
- **Daily Content:** Post daily stories to make the site feel alive.
- **Community:** Chat with authors, build communities, and add forums.

## 🌟 Future Features
- **Advanced Rating System:** Comprehensive ratings (story, writing, plot, characters, etc.).
- **AI Features:** Cover image engine, short video engine.
- **Import/Export:**
  - Google Docs integration for importing/exporting.
  - Story archive and export functionality.
- **Monetization:** Premium features (as mentioned in contest prizes).

---

## 🏆 Contest Structure
**Recommended Prize Structure (Balanced & Realistic)**

### 🥇 Grand Prize (1 winner)
**Core prizes (high impact, low cost):**
- 🏆 Featured Story on Readora (30–60 days)
- 👑 “Contest Winner” badge on profile (permanent)
- 📣 Homepage + email + socials shoutout
- 🔓 Free Premium for 1 year
- ✍️ Guaranteed placement in a “Readora Originals / Editor’s Pick” section

### 🥈 2nd–3rd Place
- ⭐ Featured story (14–30 days)
- 🏅 “Contest Finalist” badge
- 🔓 Free Premium for 6 months
- Homepage carousel placement (shorter duration)

### 🎖️ Top 10 / Shortlisted
- 🧷 “Top 10” badge
- 🔓 Free Premium for 1–3 months
- Added to a Contest Collection (permanent discoverability)

### ✨ Community-Driven Rewards (Smart Add-On Prizes)
*High ROI, low cost*
- **Reader’s Choice Award** (highest engagement)
- **Most Original Plot**
- **Best First Chapter**
- **Each gets:** Badge + featured placement + 1–3 months premium
